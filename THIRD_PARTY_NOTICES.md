# Third-party materials and release boundary

1. **LPcode**: Shinwoo Park, Hyundong Jin, Jeong-won Cha and Yo-Sub Han; https://github.com/Shinwoo-Park/LPcode ; commit `b3660c8262ae57e14498528119607ee673d4257a`. No repository LICENSE was identified during the 2026-09-05 release check. Raw data and upstream program bodies are therefore obtained directly from that repository and are not republished or relicensed here. Permission for broader reuse should be clarified with the owner.
2. **Original ten measurements**: `features_official.py` is the project's compatibility implementation of the inherited LPcode measurements. It is included byte-for-byte to preserve the frozen feature contract, with its relationship to upstream explicitly acknowledged. No claim of a newly invented endpoint feature family or independent upstream licence grant is made. Confirm applicable rights before redistribution under a permissive licence.
3. **Dependencies**: NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, XGBoost, tree-sitter and grammar packages, Matplotlib, Pillow, pytest and their dependencies remain under their own licences. Dependencies are installed from their distributors, not vendored.
4. **Publication tooling**: private Nature Figure/Codex skill code, third-party Wiley template class files and downloaded journal papers are not distributed. The public geometry helper is a newly written narrow validator and explicitly does not claim to reproduce the private skill's full collision/alignment audit. All research plots remain reproducible without that private helper.
5. **Author data**: run-level numerical records, metadata, source-data tables and figure outputs are distinguished from raw LPcode snippets. Public release is authorised by the project owner; the exact open-reuse licence awaits author confirmation in `LICENSE`.
