# Replacement for external-validity limitation
All formal results derive from one LPcode corpus, four recorded generators and four languages. No independent external benchmark evaluation has been completed. Our eligibility audit of CodeMirage did not recover the row-level ancestry needed for the paired task from its public schema. Its generation procedure also requires a separate compatibility assessment. Deterministic transformations do not cover semantic-level adversarial edits, multi-round rewrites or cross-model paraphrase chains. Generalisation claims remain bounded to the evaluated conditions.
