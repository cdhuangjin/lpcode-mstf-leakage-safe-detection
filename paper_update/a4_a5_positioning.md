# A4 and A5 positioning
A5 remains the frozen full 112-dimensional specification. A4 is the simpler 84-dimensional representation combining enhanced endpoints and signed differences. In the frozen matched ablation, clean F1 is 0.9727 for A4 and 0.9725 for A5; held-out-generator F1 rounds to 0.9671 for both. The relative block is retained transparently but should not be presented as essential.

These comparisons support a practically compact core and a near-null incremental relative-block effect in the evaluated settings. They do not establish that removing the block always helps, or that high feature importance implies a large independent or causal contribution. No external evidence changes this positioning.
