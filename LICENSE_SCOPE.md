On 5 September 2026, the project owner authorised MIT for the authors' new
code: their original MSTF software, evaluation and audit software, original
tests, release helpers and author-written software documentation.

Excluded from this grant:

- `repro/2502.17749/v1/lpcode_v1/features_official.py`, the compatibility
  implementation of inherited LPcode measurements, whose rights have not
  been confirmed for permissive relicensing;
- all other inherited or third-party code and raw LPcode snippets;
- datasets, saved results, metric pickles, numerical source tables, figures,
  manuscript text and manuscript binaries;
- third-party dependencies, fonts, publications and publisher templates.

For mixed-origin files, the grant covers only the authors' own separable
contributions. It does not establish upstream permission or override existing
notices. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and DATA_LICENSE.md. This is not a claim
that every component of the end-to-end workflow is MIT-licensed.

The v1.0.0 deposit and assets remain unchanged. This update changes no
research source, frozen result or manuscript binary.
